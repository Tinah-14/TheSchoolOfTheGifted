Why Choose School of the Gifted?

Specialized CAPS Curriculum

Students are provided with tablets integrated with the CAPS curriculum, giving them access to interactive lessons, digital textbooks, and practice exercises that make learning engaging, effective, and accessible anytime, anywhere. For learners aiming to upgrade or supplement their Grade 12 results, we offer targeted revision materials, focused exercises, and past exam papers designed to boost confidence, improve understanding, and maximize performance. Our CAT (Computer Applications Technology), and computer training programs are complemented by practical guides, interactive tutorials, and hands-on exercises, ensuring students gain both theoretical knowledge and real-world digital skills.

We build a safe, fully equipped space right in our community where students get expert tutoring, digital tools, and personal mentorship to unlock their true potential and dominate their studies. In addition to individual learning tools, our learners have access to a fully equipped study and computer lab, providing a safe and structured environment to practice, research, and explore digital technologies under the guidance of our experienced instructors.

Expert Mentorship

Learn directly from subject specialists, mathematicians, and experienced educators.

Project-Based Learning

We recognize that every learner is unique, which is why our materials and resources cater to diverse learning styles, including visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners. We are also committed to inclusivity, providing accessible resources for disadvantaged learners and learners living with disabilities, ensuring that every student has the opportunity to succeed. By combining tablets integrated with the CAPS program, hands-on materials, and access to a modern study and computer lab, the School of the Gifted empowers learners to take charge of their education, build confidence, and develop the knowledge and digital skills needed to thrive academically and in the modern world.
