About Our Academy

School of the Gifted is a dedicated learning centre offering comprehensive tutoring for students from Grade 1 to Grade 12. We provide academic support for learners who wish to improve or supplement their Grade 12 results, ensuring they achieve their full potential. Our programs include specialized tuition in CAT (Computer Applications Technology) for Grades 10 to 12, along with computer training for anyone seeking to obtain an End-User Certificate. Additionally, we offer coding and website development courses, giving students practical skills that are highly valuable in today’s digital world. At the School of the Gifted, we aim to empower every learner with knowledge, confidence, and the tools they need to succeed academically and professionally.

Our Target Audience

Our academy caters to a diverse range of learners, including students from Grade 1 to Grade 12 who are seeking to enhance their academic performance. We also welcome learners who wish to upgrade or supplement their Grade 12 results, providing them with the necessary support and resources to achieve their goals. Additionally, we offer programs for adult learners who are interested in acquiring new skills or improving their existing knowledge in areas such as computer literacy, coding, and website development. Our inclusive approach ensures that we meet the needs of all learners, regardless of age or background.

Our Mission

The mission of the School of the Gifted is to provide a comprehensive and personalized educational experience that empowers students to achieve academic excellence and develop essential 21st-century skills. We are committed to supporting learners from Grade 1 to Grade 12, including those who wish to upgrade or supplement their Grade 12 results, ensuring every student has the opportunity to reach their full potential. Our programs in CAT (Computer Applications Technology), coding, website development, and computer literacy equip students with practical digital skills that prepare them for further studies, careers, and life in an increasingly technology-driven world.

Beyond academic excellence, we are dedicated to empowering the wider community, including disadvantaged learners, and providing tailored support to people living with disabilities, ensuring that education and skill development are accessible to all. At the School of the Gifted, we foster a nurturing, inclusive, and inspiring environment that builds confidence, encourages curiosity, and nurtures lifelong learning, enabling every student and community member to thrive academically, personally, and professionally.

Our Vision

Educators are innovators.

At the School of the Gifted Academy, our mission is toempower students from grade 1 to grade 12 with exceptional educationalexperiences. We believe in nurturing their innate talents, fostering critical thinking, and promoting holistic growth. Our commitment extends beyond traditional academics to encompass character development, future readiness, and personalized support.

Our core principles include:

  • Academic Excellence: We provide rigorous and innovative curricula that challenge and inspire gifted learners. Our goal is to ignite intellectual curiosity and cultivate a lifelong love for learning.
  • Individualized Support: For students who faced challenges in their final year, we offer supplementary classes tailored to their specific needs. Our dedicated educators work closely with each student to bridge gaps and ensure their success.
  • Community Empowerment: We actively engage with the community, providing educational resources and support to disadvantaged learners. Our outreach programs aim to uplift and empower individuals, fostering a culture of inclusivity and social responsibility.
  • Empowering People Living with Disabilities: We are committed to creating an inclusive learning environment that accommodates the unique needs of students with disabilities. Our programs are designed to provide equal opportunities for growth and achievement.
