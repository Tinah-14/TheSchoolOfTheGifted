Educational Programs & Offerings

Our academy offers a range of specialized programs designed to nurture gifted learners in CAPS. Each program is structured to provide hands-on experience, mentorship, and project-based learning opportunities.

Gifted students coding in STEM lab
  • 1.Academic Tutoring from Grade 1-12

    • Comprehensive tutoring in all subjects for students from Grade 1 to Grade 12, including CAPS-aligned curriculum support.

    Schedule: Monday to Friday (15:00 - 18:00) | Tuition: R400 / month to R550 / month

  • 2.Supplementary Grade 12 Results

    • Focused support for learners seeking to upgrade or supplement their Grade 12 results, with targeted revision and exam preparation.

    Schedule: Mondays to Saturdays (09:00 - 15:00) | Tuition: R500 / month

  • 3.MICT Programs

    • 6 months comprehensive programs for learners interested in multimedia and information communication technologies, including web development, digital design, and software engineering, an accredited certificate with SETA will be issued upon completion. A matric certificate will be required upon application.

    Schedule: Mondays to Fridays (07:00 - 14:00) | Tuition: R3 167 / month
